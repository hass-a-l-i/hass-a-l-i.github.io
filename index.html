About

The H-Space takes its inspiration from the concept of the Hilbert Space, or at least a represenation of it applied to human thought. It seems to me that such a name is appropriate for a variety of reasons. A human thought can be finite in how it is layered against other thougts (dimensionality), or can appear to have an infite number of other dependent thoughts branching from it. Hilbert Spaces are also commonly defined as being over the complex space of numbers, much like how human thoughts can become quite complex. Similarly, Hilbert Spaces can contain real numbers - akin to a thought or idea becoming something man-made, tangible, with inputs and outputs. My H-Space is then simply a type of Hilbert Space but for my own thoughts, defined over the spaces of complex thoughts and real projects, where the dimensionality of each thought is free to exist in a (seemly) infinite space.

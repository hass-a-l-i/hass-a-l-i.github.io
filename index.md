My name is Hassan and I use this space share my thoughts and personal passions with other like-minded individuals. My interests are constantly changing as I thoroughly enjoy learning about new things! At the moment my interests lie in the following topics:

- Mathematics

- Economics

- Psychology

I'm sure there are more but that should cover the majority. Alogside these posts - I enjoy exchanging thoughts in the marketplace of ideas. It is my firm belief that thought provoking questions and meaningful discourse allow us to understand one another more clearly. As a result, the mental barriers we put in place that divide us can momentarily dissolve, allowing us to truly appreciate the perspective of the other human being we are conversing with. At a time where the world seems so divided, it seems that this rekindling of the marketplace of ideas into the respected space it once was is sorely needed.

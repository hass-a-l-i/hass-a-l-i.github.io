## A secondary purpose
Alogside these posts, I enjoy exchanging thoughts with others in the marketplace of ideas. It is my firm belief that thought provoking questions and meaningful discourse allow us to understand one another more clearly. As a result, the mental barriers we build between ourselves momentarily dissolve, allowing us to appreciate the perspective of what is simply another human being with a different set of experiences which mould their worldview. At a time where the world seems so divided, rekindling the marketplace of ideas into the respected space seems more important than ever. My writing on H space intends to achieve exactly that.
